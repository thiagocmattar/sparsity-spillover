# 001: Forty 14M endpoints, including h/z thresholds

## Question

Where do the four Run041 h/z threshold endpoints lie in the same quality-sparsity
and full-model latency views as all models in the current Figure14 panel (a)?

## Figure and method

[PDF](../figures/01-14m-quality-sparsity-latency-with-hz.pdf), generated by
[`01_build.py`](../01_build.py). Exact data, source identities, styles, visible
limits and exclusions are in [`figure-data.json`](../data/figure-data.json).
Figure06 supplies the side-by-side quality/latency layout; Figure14 supplies the
exact historical cohort. Both source PDFs remain unchanged.

All 36 selected historical trained records are retained without coordinate
changes. Four additional Run041 endpoints are joined to their final checkpoint
identities. Ordinary final-checkpoint loss is used for all 40 models; the
nearby eager logical-pass loss is not substituted. Model-wide sparsity pools
actual zero-product counts over all six counted block operation families and
divides by the block-plus-dense-LM-head product denominator. Integer sums are
reconciled against the original logical artifacts before plotting.

All models share the pinned 14M architecture, random initialization bytes,
model/data seed 1234, realized training order, 712 updates and 1,493,172,224 input
tokens. The retained artifact records verify these identities. Validation uses
all 338 complete 2,048-token blocks from 500 MiniPile documents: 692,224 input
tokens; the 1,444-token tail is excluded. Latency is full-model K050 host time
on RTX5090, BF16, B=1, T=2048, uncached full-vocabulary inference. Each point
aggregates 64 inputs x 7 passes x 3 fresh processes (1,344 samples) geometrically.

## Caption and legend

**Quality, model-wide sparsity and full-model latency on Pythia-14M.**
(a) Ordinary final-checkpoint validation loss versus count-pooled model-wide
logical sparsity. (b) K050 latency for the same 40 trained checkpoints.
The 36 models from Figure14 panel (a) are augmented with four dark-blue
`T_hz/P_h` endpoints: one-sided threshold gates at h and z, h-only OL1,
lambda=1, kappa=0/.01/.05/.1. All other pressured recipes also use OL1.
T4/T7 lines connect separate trained thresholds kappa=0/.01/.05/.1/.5;
T1/P1 connects pressure weights lambda=.05/.1/.5/1. Controls use larger
open gray (Base) and filled olive (ReLU) circles. Existing colors and line
styles identify the other recipes. Lines connect measured settings without
interpolation claims or coordinate jitter.

Dotted gray/olive paths in (a) retain the ten existing post-hoc magnitude-clipping
settings for each fixed Base/ReLU checkpoint at a,m,h,z. Eight high-loss settings
continue above the view; all twenty are retained in the data export. Run041 is
unclipped. The latency panel contains trained models only, following Figure06;
control-clipping latency was separately measured in Run036. Vertical guides in
(a) mark analytic h/z, T4 and T7 reach ceilings (5.3471%, 12.8332%, 29.9524%),
which are neither measured zero fractions nor speedup limits. All forty trained
points fit the displayed ranges in both panels.

## Result

| Run041 kappa | Validation loss | Model-wide sparsity | K050 latency (ms) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 5.128602 | 4.631224% | 0.612348 |
| 0.01 | 5.130649 | 4.717262% | 0.608618 |
| 0.05 | 5.134379 | 5.003663% | 0.590124 |
| 0.1 | 5.151098 | 5.174573% | 0.573427 |

The new series occupies a low-loss region at 4.63%-5.17% model-wide sparsity.
Within Run041, increasing kappa raises loss and logical sparsity while reducing
measured K050 latency. Every Run041 process passes full-validation qualification,
with zero recorded logit absolute/relative-L2 error and pooled loss difference
against its BF16 eager-native reference. This does not equate the separately
recorded FP16 quality loss with BF16 qualification loss.

## Limits and verification

The figure uses one training seed and a fixed training budget. Joint h/z gates
and h pressure do not isolate either site's causal contribution. Geometric
means summarize timing samples, not training-seed replicates or uncertainty.
The Run029, Run033 and Run041 sessions contribute 31, 5 and 4 plotted models,
respectively, on different physical GPU/host allocations. Small cross-session
latency differences do not establish a controlled ranking. No normalization,
fitted frontier, loss adjustment or speedup inference is introduced.

The user explicitly retained panel (a)'s exclusion of the four naive-L1 models;
their original records are listed under `excluded_naive_l1` in the export.
Clipping coverage and visible exclusions match the reference quality panel.
The source builder reconciles all 40 losses, integer logical counts and final
checkpoint identities, all 20 clipping coordinates, all 12 Run041 process
qualifications and raw timing means, and all 149 source hashes. PDF page count,
embedded fonts, text bounds, reference-file preservation and visual QA are
recorded in [`verification.json`](../data/verification.json) and
[`visual-review.json`](../data/visual-review.json). No manuscript
text or finding is updated by this analysis.
