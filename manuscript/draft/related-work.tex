\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\subsection{Sparsification interventions}
\label{sec:related-interventions}

CATS thresholds small FFN activations in training-free and fine-tuning
settings~\citep{lee2024cats}; TEAL extends magnitude thresholding to attention
and FFN projections with block-wise greedy allocation~\citep{liu2024teal}.
Our uniform clipping reference does not test TEAL's optimized allocation.
Training can also reshape the trade-off: ReLU Strikes Back restores sparse
activations~\citep{mirzadeh2023relu}; ProSparse combines ReLU, progressive
$\ell_1$ pressure and threshold shifting, with schedule ablations showing
different quality at comparable FFN sparsity~\citep{song2024prosparse}.
Sparsing Law studies data, activation and architecture effects~\citep{luo2025sparsing},
and \citet{cetin2026sparser} induce sparse FFNs through pretraining regularization.

Beyond FFNs, Q-Sparse applies top-$k$ selection to attention and FFN
projection inputs and ablates its nonlinearity and straight-through
estimator~\citep{wang2024qsparse}. Spark combines sparse FFNs with
attention-position selection and studies its predictor~\citep{you2025spark}.
Our seven-site intervention instead also thresholds feature coordinates
inside attention. We examine pressure's conditional effect and the resulting
operation-weighted counts, with and without pressure at 14M.

\subsection{Inference speedup}
\label{sec:related-speedup}

TEAL and Spark report autoregressive decoding gains~\citep{liu2024teal,you2025spark}.
TwELL targets batched matrix multiplication with packed sparse activations
and fused FFNs, evaluating full-sequence throughput~\citep{cetin2026sparser}.
Workload and structure matter: different token masks affect batching savings;
Block Q-Sparse structures masks for batched execution~\citep{wang2024qsparse}.
Our case study uses execution and profiling feedback for kernel
development~\citep{ouyang2025kernelbench,luo2026agentic}, then matched fusion
and skipping controls to identify what produces full-model acceleration.
