\section{Experimental study}
\label{sec:experiments}

We pretrain Pythia models from random initialization on
MiniPile~\citep{biderman2023pythia,kaddour2023minipile}: 30 conditions at 14M
and 12 each at 70M and 410M. Each trains for 712 updates and 1.493 billion
input tokens, with one training seed; we evaluate the final checkpoint.
This common budget does not imply the same training stage at each size.
Loss is next-token cross-entropy (lower is better), measured with $\Smodel$
over all 338 complete 2,048-token blocks from 500 validation documents;
the 1,444-token tail is excluded. Appendix~\ref{app:training-protocol}
gives all settings, including the lower 410M learning rate.

Within each size, paired conditions share initialization, data order and
budget; reused controls are not independent replications. Pressure on/off
contrasts hold nonlinearities and threshold fixed. Unpressured A7 versus A4
adds symmetric $q,k,v$ thresholding; A7-OL1 versus A4-OL1 changes complete
recipes, including the target-gradient mixture and cap-dependent scale. A4 versus A1-H also changes
$h$'s threshold above zero and its derivative at zero. Local pressure varies
$\lambda\in\{0.05,0.1,0.5,1\}$; A4/A7 vary
$\kappa\in\{0,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.5\}$. Multisite OL1 uses $\lambda=1$;
all OL1 conditions use trust budget 1. Larger models use A0, A1-H, A4-OL1
and A7-OL1; Figure~\ref{fig:intervention-ladder} lists all eight recipes.

We also apply post-hoc clipping to fixed trained checkpoints, including
GELU A0 and ReLU A1-H. This second intervention uses uniform magnitude clipping at
$a,m,h,z$, calibrated separately per site/layer on ten training blocks,
for targets $p=0,0.1,\ldots,0.9$. Target $p$ is a quantile, not achieved
$\Smodel$. This TEAL-style control does not optimize allocation~\citep{liu2024teal}.
It acts after the trained nonlinearity, preserving A0's GELU or A1-H's ReLU, whereas A7
replaces GELU and also thresholds internal attention. Thus the comparison
changes reach and final transformations as well as intervention timing.
Appendix~\ref{app:clipping-results} retains all 540 evaluations.
