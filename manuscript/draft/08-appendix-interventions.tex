\subsection{Activation pressure and OL1}
\label{app:pressure}

\textbf{Objective and update.}
For $J$ activation tensors $A_j$ at the sites and layers targeted by
$\mathcal P$, each with $n_j$ entries after any thresholding, define
\begin{equation}
\ell_1(\theta;\mathcal P)=\frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\frac{1}{n_j}
\sum_{i=1}^{n_j}|A_{j,i}|.
\end{equation}
Each tensor has equal weight, so changing $\mathcal P$ changes both the
targets and normalization. Ordinary L1 applies AdamW~\citep{loshchilov2019adamw}
to the globally clipped gradient of $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{task}}+\lambda\ell_1$.
OL1 separates task and pressure updates: task-only AdamW is followed by a
pressure correction that removes the opposing component and limits its norm.
Pressure therefore does not directly enter Adam's moment estimates.

Both gradients are evaluated at the same parameters and averaged over the
same microbatches. The clipped task gradient updates AdamW's bias-corrected
moments $\widehat m_t,\widehat v_t$; the pressure gradient
$g_1=\nabla_\theta\ell_1$ is unclipped and has no momentum. Using the same
preconditioner for both directions gives
\begin{equation}
u=\frac{\widehat m_t}{\sqrt{\widehat v_t}+\epsilon_A},
\qquad
w=\frac{g_1}{\sqrt{\widehat v_t}+\epsilon_A}.
\end{equation}
Norms and inner products include parameters with both gradients and initialized
Adam state. For $c=\langle u,w\rangle$, $q=\|u\|_2^2$ and stabilizer $\epsilon>0$,
\begin{equation}
\widetilde w=
\begin{cases}
w-\dfrac{c}{q+\epsilon}u, & c<0\ \text{and}\ q>\epsilon,\\[3pt]
w, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\label{eq:ol1-projection}
\end{equation}
Aligned pressure is retained. For weight $\lambda\geq0$ and relative norm budget $b>0$,
\begin{equation}
r=\frac{\lambda\|\widetilde w\|_2}{\|u\|_2+\epsilon},
\qquad
s=\min\!\left\{1,\frac{b}{r+\epsilon}\right\}\quad(r>0),
\end{equation}
with $s=1$ when $r=0$. After task-only AdamW, each parameter group $p$ receives
$\theta_p\leftarrow\theta_p-\alpha_p\lambda s\widetilde w_p$, where
$\alpha_p$ is its learning rate. Before learning-rate scaling and ignoring
stabilizers, the correction norm is
$\min\{\lambda\|\widetilde w\|_2,b\|u\|_2\}$. At a fixed optimizer state,
increasing $\lambda$ has no further effect once the cap is active.

OL1 combines conflict projection~\citep{yu2020pcgrad} and a smoothed task
direction~\citep{hsieh2024bloop} in Adam's preconditioned coordinates.
The stabilizer makes orthogonality approximate. This calculation excludes
group learning rates and decoupled weight decay; avoiding opposition to the
Adam direction does not guarantee unchanged task or validation loss.

\textbf{L1 versus OL1.}
At 14M $T_1/P_1$, matched initialization, data order and training budget yield
similar results (Table~\ref{tab:l1-ol1}). Sparsity differs by less than
$0.02$ percentage points at every $\lambda$. OL1 has slightly lower loss at
$\lambda\in\{0.05,0.1,0.5\}$ and higher loss at $\lambda=1$.
One seed cannot establish whether these small loss differences persist across seeds.
