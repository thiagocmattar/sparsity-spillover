\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Training-free and trained sparsity.}
TEAL removes low-magnitude activations and allocates sparsity across a model
using a blockwise procedure \citep{liu2024teal}. CATS studies activation
thresholding \citep{lee2024cats}. ReLU-based models, progressive regularization,
and sparse pretraining establish that training can accommodate substantial
feed-forward sparsity
\citep{mirzadeh2023relu,song2024prosparse,zhang2024exploring}.
The Sparsing Law studies how activation sparsity relates to model size, data,
and quality \citep{liu2024sparsing}. Our uniform, calibrated clipping baseline
provides a fixed-model reference for the same checkpoints and validation
coverage. TEAL's optimized allocation and the full training recipes of these
methods remain important comparisons.

\paragraph{Sparsity across operations.}
Q-Sparse is a close precedent: it applies top-$k$ sparsification to linear
inputs in attention and feed-forward blocks, supports block sparsity, and
uses a straight-through estimator during training \citep{wang2024qsparse}.
Spark introduces top-$k$ feed-forward and attention sparsity and evaluates
execution \citep{you2025spark}; recent work also combines sparse model design
with inference kernels \citep{cetin2026sparser}. Our study examines a
different set of matched interventions: fixed-threshold gates with their
ordinary masked gradients, explicit magnitude pressure, and additional gates
on the actual query, key, and value operands. Queries and keys are gated
after rotary position encoding. The evidence addresses these design choices
in Pythia pretraining; direct comparisons with top-$k$ and structured
training are needed to establish their relative advantages.

\paragraph{Task-aware auxiliary optimization.}
Gradient surgery modifies auxiliary updates when objectives conflict
\citep{yu2020pcgrad,mitrevski2024bloop}. We use a task-aware magnitude-pressure
rule, denoted OL1, alongside a conventional joint-loss L1 baseline.
The OL1 recipe maintains task-only Adam moments, projects conflicting
preconditioned pressure updates, and bounds their norm. Its role is to supply
a consistent optimization intervention across gate configurations.
The matched comparisons evaluate the complete rule; its individual
components would require separate ablations.
