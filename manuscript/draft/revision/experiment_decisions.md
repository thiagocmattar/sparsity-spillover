Before any package, freeze primary contrasts, seeds, checkpoint-selection rule,
full validation coverage, objective coefficients and diagnostic inventory.
Consider exact/near-zero counts, RMS/L2, weight norms, gradient conflict and
trust-budget binding, actual logical products, clipping thresholds and final
checkpoint retention. Specify the full package cost, including controls,
replications, calibration, implementation, storage and transfer, after choosing
the design and obtaining current resource prices. No ETC or GPU cost is
invented here. Research AGENTS.md requires separate design and launch approvals.

The highest-value next investment for a stronger empirical claim is focused
M1/M2 work. M3 matters if broad post-hoc superiority is retained; H1/H2 should
not replace missing basic controls. Favorable results are not required: an
identified reversal can be informative. Stop expanding a headline if only
small unstable effects remain. The existing-only rewrite cannot resolve
training variability or untested component attribution.
