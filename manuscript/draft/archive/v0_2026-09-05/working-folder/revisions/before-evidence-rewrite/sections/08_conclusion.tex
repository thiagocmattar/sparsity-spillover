\section{Conclusion}

Activation sparsity becomes scientifically comparable only after declaring
where zeros meet products.  $\Rmodel$ counts that opportunity across the causal
transformer graph while keeping local sites and operation contributions
inspectable.  Across three Pythia scales, broad attention gating can create
substantially more logical opportunity than highly sparse MLP-adjacent sites,
but quality responses depend on intervention and training regime.  The 410M
reversal prevents a premature scaling story and sharpens the next empirical
question: whether trained quality--opportunity behavior persists under matched
optimization maturity.  Until sparse kernels are measured, the result remains
a map of exact-zero products, not a speed claim.
