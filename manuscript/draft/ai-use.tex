\section*{AI use statement}

AI assistance supported code for the reported reductions, inspection of
experiment records, interpretation of results, literature checks, preparation
of figures and tables, and drafting and editing this manuscript. The kernel
case study additionally used a human-guided coding-agent loop for implementation,
testing and optimization. Its recorded default model was \emph{gpt-6-astra};
per-request served-model identities were not verified. Reported measurements
are tied to retained execution records. Numerical reductions were checked
against source counts and timing samples, and kernel claims use the
qualification procedure in Appendix~\ref{app:kernel-results}.
