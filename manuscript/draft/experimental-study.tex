\section{Experimental study}
\label{sec:experiments}

We pretrain Pythia-14M, 70M, and 410M from random initialization~\citep{biderman2023pythia} on MiniPile~\citep{kaddour2023minipile}, using 1.493 billion input tokens per model. We evaluate validation loss and $\Smodel$ over all complete validation sequences. Table~\ref{tab:interventions} defines the thresholding and OL1-pressure recipes and their sparsity ceilings.

Within each size, runs share initialization, data order and training budget.
For $T_4/T_7$, we sweep $\kappa\in\{0,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.5\}$ with
$P_0/P_h/P_{\mathrm{all}}$ at 14M, $P_h/P_{\mathrm{all}}$ at 70M, and
$P_{\mathrm{all}}$ at 410M, using $\lambda=b=1$. All sizes include
$T_0/P_0$ and $T_1/P_0$ controls. At 14M, $T_1/P_1$ uses
$\lambda\in\{0.05,0.1,0.5,1\}$. Including the historical naive-L1 controls
reported in the appendix, the study contains 40/22/12 conditions at
14M/70M/410M (74 total). Appendix~\ref{app:training-protocol} gives the full protocol.

To compare the interventions with post-hoc thresholding, we apply uniform percentile magnitude-thresholding targets $p=0,0.1,\ldots,0.9$ at $a,m,h,z$ as a TEAL-style baseline~\citep{liu2024teal}. Site--layer thresholds are calibrated separately on ten training blocks.
