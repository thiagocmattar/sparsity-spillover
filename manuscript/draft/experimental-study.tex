\section{Experimental study}
\label{sec:experiments}

We pretrain randomly initialized Pythia-14M, 70M, and 410M
models~\citep{biderman2023pythia}. To fit our compute budget, we adapt
Pythia's training recipe to MiniPile~\citep{kaddour2023minipile}, using
1.493 billion input tokens per model. We conduct extensive comparisons at 14M and promote the sparsest recipes
to 70M and 410M, alongside their baselines.
We measure validation cross-entropy and $\Smodel$ in the same forward passes
over all complete sequences from MiniPile's 500 validation documents.
All training-result figures and tables use this evaluation.
Table~\ref{tab:interventions} maps the eight recipes onto the sites in
Figure~\ref{fig:pythia-block}.

Within each size, matched contrasts share initialization, data order, and
training budget. Adding pressure at fixed nonlinearities measures its additional
quality and sparsity effects; repeating the contrast across thresholds tests
how those effects depend on thresholding. Comparing A4 with A7 without
pressure examines broader nonlinearity placement. A4-OL1 versus A7-OL1 changes both
nonlinearity and pressure sites, so it compares complete recipes rather than isolating
nonlinearity placement at a fixed pressure objective.

The cohort contains 30 conditions at 14M and 12 at each larger size.
Each trains for 712 updates and 1.493 billion
input tokens; we evaluate the final checkpoint. Local pressure varies
$\lambda\in\{0.05,0.1,0.5,1\}$; A4/A7 vary
$\kappa\in\{0,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.5\}$. Multisite OL1 uses $\lambda=1$,
and all OL1 conditions use trust budget 1. Sweeping these settings tests
whether an added intervention has a consistent effect or depends on the
strength of the existing intervention. Appendix~\ref{app:training-protocol} gives the
optimizer, precision and learning-rate settings.

For the evaluation-only reference, we apply uniform magnitude-clipping targets
$p=0,0.1,\ldots,0.9$ at $a,m,h,z$ without updating weights. Site--layer
thresholds are calibrated separately on ten training blocks. This is a
uniform TEAL-style control~\citep{liu2024teal}, not a reproduction of an
optimized allocation across sites. The main figures show A0 clipping;
Appendix~\ref{app:clipping-results} includes all 540 evaluations across the
54 trained checkpoints.
