\section{Experimental study}
\label{sec:experiments}

We pretrain randomly initialized Pythia-14M, 70M, and 410M models~\citep{biderman2023pythia}. To fit our compute budget, we adapt Pythia's training recipe to MiniPile~\citep{kaddour2023minipile}, using 1.493 billion input tokens per model. We conduct extensive comparisons at 14M and promote the sparsest recipes to 70M and 410M, alongside their baselines. We measure validation cross-entropy loss and $\Smodel$ over all complete sequences from MiniPile's validation documents. Table~\ref{tab:interventions} maps the eight recipes onto the site types in Figure~\ref{fig:pythia-block}.

The experimental setup contains 30 training runs at 14M and 12 at each larger model size. Within each size, runs share initialization, data order and training budget. At 14M, we vary local pressure for A1-H using L1N and OL1 with $\lambda\in\{0.05,0.1,0.5,1\}$. For A4/A7, we sweep $\kappa\in\{0,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.5\}$, both without pressure and with OL1 at $\lambda=b=1$. Figure~\ref{fig:ol1-geometry} characterizes the resulting cap activity at 14M. There is one training seed per condition, and all quality comparisons reuse the same validation split. Conditions are not independent-seed replications. The larger cohorts contain only the pressure-on multisite sweeps; they do not isolate the effect of adding pressure. Appendix~\ref{app:training-protocol} details the training settings.

For post-hoc magnitude thresholding, we zero activations below uniform percentile targets $p=0,0.1,\ldots,0.9$ at $a,m,h,z$ as a TEAL-style baseline~\citep{liu2024teal}. Site--layer thresholds are calibrated separately on ten training blocks.
