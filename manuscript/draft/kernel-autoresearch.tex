\subsection{Realizing sparsity with specialized inference kernels}
\label{sec:kernel-autoresearch}

Which counted zeros improve full-model latency after separating fusion from
skipping overhead? We compare specialized kernels with native PyTorch/SDPA
CUDA graphs on one RTX5090: BF16, batch one, uncached 2,048-token inference
with full vocabulary logits. Qualification checks logits and loss within
specified tolerances on all 338 validation blocks. The selected K050
qualifies on all 30 checkpoints; Appendix~\ref{app:kernel-results} retains
the development history, TwELL compatibility and numerical contract.

Speedup is native latency divided by candidate latency in paired timing
samples. K050 achieves a $1.2340\times$ equal-checkpoint geometric mean;
the same implementation with all skipping disabled gives $1.1829\times$.
The ratio of these separately measured native-relative speedups defines
the sparse-path factor: $1.0432\times$ geometrically, benefiting 14 of 30
checkpoints. At the fixed A7-OL1, $\kappa=0.5$ search checkpoint, the
retrospective reaches $1.7830\times$ over native and sparse paths add
$1.311\times$ over the matched control. This factor is not a direct paired
latency ratio between implementations; Appendix~\ref{app:runtime-sensitivity}
retains the separate final sweep and descriptive association checks.


For example, unpressured A4 and A7 at $\kappa=0.5$ have specialized
latencies of 0.4790 and 0.4789\,ms, but BF16 losses of 5.6623 and 5.7076.
A7 has more counted zeros and a larger native-relative speedup, yet the
absolute latencies are almost identical and its loss is higher. Different
native baselines can enlarge a normalized speedup without identifying a
better intervention on the quality--latency plane.

Disabling attention skipping improves every checkpoint, raising the
native-relative geometric mean to $1.2506\times$. K050 bypasses an
instruction when either complete operand fragment is zero; at the search
checkpoint it skips $58.0\%$ of eligible $QK$ and $67.2\%$ of $PV$
instructions, yet is slower than dense attention. Partial zeros can still
require the instruction, while detection and control cost time. Architectural
reach justifies investigating attention; profitable execution additionally
depends on fragment granularity, shape and overhead in this workload.
