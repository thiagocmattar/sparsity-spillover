## Interpretation limits retained

- Higher-threshold projection gains are not claimed to increase strictly at
  every adjacent threshold or to benefit every checkpoint. Four-site OL1's
  increment is positive at the four positive thresholds, not at zero.
- MMA bypass can substitute scalar work for matrix instructions. It is not
  automatically arithmetic eliminated; padding and coverage differ from S_model.
- Projection gain divides full-model candidate latencies with attention
  skipping disabled in both controls. It is not projection-only timing.
- Native-relative speedup can grow while absolute K050 latency also grows.
- Attention skipping is slower for every checkpoint here, not intrinsically
  unprofitable on other shapes, hardware or implementations.
- The agent configuration does not prove the model served for every request,
  and there is no human-only development comparison.
