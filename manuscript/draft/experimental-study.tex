\section{Experimental study}
\label{sec:experiments}

We pretrain randomly initialized Pythia-14M, 70M, and 410M models~\citep{biderman2023pythia}. To fit our compute budget, we adapt Pythia's training recipe to MiniPile~\citep{kaddour2023minipile}, using 1.493 billion input tokens per model. We conduct extensive comparisons at 14M and promote the sparsest recipes to 70M and 410M, alongside their baselines. We measure validation cross-entropy loss and $\Smodel$ over all complete sequences from MiniPile's validation documents. Table~\ref{tab:interventions} maps the ten recipes onto the sites in Figure~\ref{fig:pythia-block}.

The experimental setup contains 40 training runs at 14M and 12 at each larger model size, for 64 endpoints. Within each size, runs share initialization, data order and training budget. We vary local pressure for A1-H using L1N and OL1 with $\lambda\in\{0.05,0.1,0.5,1\}$. At 14M, we sweep $\kappa\in\{0,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.5\}$ under both threshold scopes, with no pressure, h-only OL1, or OL1 at all thresholded sites. Multisite OL1 uses $\lambda=b=1$. The larger sizes retain only the broad-pressure sweeps. Figure~\ref{fig:ol1-geometry} characterizes the resulting cap activity at 14M. These sweeps test which interventions yield the largest sparsification gains. Appendix~\ref{app:training-protocol} details the training settings.

To compare the interventions with post-hoc thresholding, we apply uniform percentile magnitude-thresholding targets $p=0,0.1,\ldots,0.9$ at $a,m,h,z$ as a TEAL-style baseline~\citep{liu2024teal}. Site--layer thresholds are calibrated separately on ten training blocks.
