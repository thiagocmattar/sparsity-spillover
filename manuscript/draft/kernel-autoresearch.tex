\subsection{When model-wide sparsity translates to speedup}
\label{sec:activation-reshaping}
\label{sec:kernel-autoresearch}

Sparsity helps when skipping saves more work than detecting zeros costs.
We measure \emph{bypass} as the number of skipped matrix-multiply instructions
divided by the total issued plus skipped instructions for each operation;
it is not the fraction of that operation's running time removed.
Table~\ref{tab:kernel-mechanisms} separates these counts from measured latency.
At $h,z$, the kernel inspects $8\times16$ activation groups before requesting
their corresponding weights: empty groups avoid both weight reads and matrix
instructions, while eligible rows with at most two nonzeros use direct scalar products.
For $T_4/P_h$, projection skipping gives a $1.37\times$ full-model gain with
bypass confined to $h,z$. At $a,m$, the kernel instead checks $16\times16$
groups after loading both operands, saving only matrix instructions;
$T_7/P_{\mathrm{all}}$ bypasses just 8.4\% and 0\%, respectively.

Attention uses FlashAttention's tiled computation \citep{dao2022flashattention}:
load $Q,K$ tiles, form scores, apply masking and softmax, then multiply
probabilities by $V$. Our zero checks occur after operand loads, within these
tile computations. Consequently, discovering an empty tile saves its matrix
multiplication, but does not recover the loading work already performed.
Softmax still runs and maps zero scores to nonzero probabilities, so $Q/K$
sparsity does not automatically propagate to $PV$; zeros in $V$ remain usable.
Sparsifying the probabilities themselves is a further opportunity, untested here.

For $T_7/P_{\mathrm{all}}$, substantial $QK/PV$ bypass therefore coexists with
a net slowdown: enabling attention skipping raises latency from 0.468 to
0.473\,ms with projection skipping fixed. The net cost of the attention skip
path outweighs its arithmetic savings in all 35 matched 14M comparisons.
These timings isolate projection and attention paths, but neither individual
sites nor each source of overhead; they do not establish the same attribution
at 70M (Appendix~\ref{app:kernel-diagnostics}).
